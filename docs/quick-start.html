brim quick start

Your bath tells your phone before it overflows. About 10 minutes to set up.

brimtub bracket2 suction cupsany flat Qi charging pad (yours)the free ntfy app on your phone
Step 1 · on the sofa

Connect it to your Wi-Fi

  1. Put brim on your Qi pad for a moment to wake it. Its light blinks cyan.
  2. Scan the Wi-Fi QR code on the card in the box, or join brim-setup-XXXX with the password printed on it. The setup page opens by itself (if not, go to 192.168.4.1).
  3. Pick your home Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz) and type its password.
  4. Tap Copy topic. In the ntfy app tap +, paste, subscribe.
  5. Tap Save. Within a minute ntfy shows “brim is connected” and the light flashes green twice.

Cyan again, no alert? The password was wrong. Rejoin brim-setup: the page says what failed.

Step 2 · at the tub

Mount the bracket at your “full” line

  1. Push each suction cup's head through the round end of a keyhole and slide it to the top of the slot.
  2. Decide how full you like your bath. Wipe the tub wall, wet the cups, and press the bracket on so its top edge sits 3 mm below that line.
  3. Slide brim in from the top, logo facing into the tub, flat Qi side against the wall.

The two small arrows on brim's face, and the groove on each side, now sit at your full line. The grey panels you can see through the case are the level sensors: the bath is tracked from the lowest one up.

Step 3 · every bath

Run the bath. brim does the rest.

It wakes when water reaches the lowest sensor, then blips dim blue every 4 s while it watches. Your phone gets:

Nearly fullAbout 2 minutes to go.
Bath fullUrgent. brim also flashes red and beeps. Repeats every minute while still rising, up to 15 min.
Bath too hotAbove 38 °C, set for little ones. Change it in settings (40 or 42 °C for older kids and adults), and add an optional “cooling down” alert.
Battery and check-ins“Battery low” below 20 %. A silent “brim OK” once a week, and “brim hasn’t checked in” if it goes quiet for 3 days.

Double-tap brim to silence a bath-full alarm: two green flashes and a chirp. Also, if brim gets knocked out of its bracket you'll get “brim is tilted”.

Step 4 · about every 6 months

Charge it flat, like a phone

  1. Pinch the grip grooves near the top and lift brim out of the bracket. The bracket stays on the tub.
  2. Lay it back down on a flat Qi pad, logo up, the printed “Qi” ring over the pad's centre. Vertical phone stands put their coil too high. No pad? A Samsung Galaxy with Wireless PowerShare or a Pixel with Battery Share works: lay brim back to back with the phone.
  3. Light breathes amber while charging, turns steady green when full (about 2½ hours).

Each time it lands on the pad you get a silent “brim is charging” notification. Tap it within 10 minutes to open the settings page: change Wi-Fi, alarm level or hot limit, or send a test alert. To save changes, first double-tap brim on the pad (two green flashes): changes stay unlocked for 2 minutes, so nobody else on your network can change them.

What the light means

The light glows through the case. Tap or pick up a sleeping brim to see its battery.

Cyan blink: waiting for setup
Blue blip every 4 s: watching a bath
Yellow breathing: nearly full
Red fast blink + beeps: bath full
Green steady: alarm silenced, or charged
Amber breathing: charging
Magenta breathing: water too hot
Orange double blink: tilted or out of bracket
1 s flash: battery > 50 % / > 20 % / lower

If something's off

No “brim is connected” alert
Check you subscribed to the exact topic. If the light blinks cyan again, the Wi-Fi password was wrong or the network is 5 GHz only.
Want to check alerts still work
Put brim on the pad, tap “brim is charging”, then Send a test alert.
Moved house or changed Wi-Fi
Same: on the pad, tap the notification, double-tap brim, edit the Wi-Fi.
Page says “Locked”
Double-tap brim on its pad, then press Save again within 2 minutes.
Start over, or giving it away
Put brim on the pad and lift it off 3 times within 20 seconds. It flashes red, forgets everything (including your Wi-Fi password) and blinks cyan for setup.
Bracket slipping
Clean the tile or acrylic, re-wet the cups and press them out from the centre. Avoid textured or grouted spots.

brim is a reminder, not a lifeguard. Never leave a child or anyone at risk alone in the bath. Keep the Qi pad (a mains device) away from water, and only charge brim dry and out of the tub.
