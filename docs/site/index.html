Has this ever happened to you?

The bath's running, the phone rings or the kids need you, and you come back to water lapping over the rim. brim sits on your tub wall and taps your phone a couple of minutes before the bath is full, then again when it gets there. It also warns you if the water's too hot.

Set up once

Ten minutes to set up, then you forget it's there.

  1. Stick it at your full line

    Two suction cups hold the bracket on the inside of the tub. Put its top a few millimetres below the level you like. brim slides in from the top.

  2. Join your Wi-Fi

    Your phone joins brim's setup network and a page opens by itself. Pick your Wi-Fi, subscribe in the free ntfy app, and you'll get a “brim is connected” alert.

  3. Run baths as usual

    brim wakes when the water reaches it and watches the level rise. If something pulls your attention away, your phone reminds you to turn off the tap.

What's inside

Sealed shut. Senses straight through the wall.

No holes, no buttons, no ports. Everything happens through 1.2 mm of plastic.

6 levels · 60 mm

Measures, doesn't guess

Six capacitive pads read the water through the case, plus a dry reference pad that cancels drift. Suds and splashes don't fool it.

±0.1 °C sensor

Too-hot warning

A medical-grade temperature sensor reads the water through a thin thermal window. Pick your limit, 42 °C by default.

Qi

Charges like a phone

Lay it on any flat Qi pad. One charge lasts about four months of daily baths.

ntfy

Alerts without an account

Notifications go through ntfy, a free open-source app. No brim account, no subscription, and you can run your own server.

light + beeper

Works when Wi-Fi doesn't

brim also flashes and beeps on its own, so a full bath gets noticed even if your phone is in another room.

double-tap

One gesture

Double-tap the case to silence an alarm. Tap a sleeping brim to see its battery colour.

One bath, start to finish

It watches the rise, so it can warn you early.

  1. Water touches the lowest padbrim wakes up

    A dim blue blip every few seconds means it's watching.

  2. About 2 minutes outBath nearly full

    It measures how fast the water is rising and works out when it'll reach your line.

  3. At your lineBATH FULL

    An urgent alert, plus a red light and beeps. It repeats each minute while the water keeps rising, up to 15 minutes, unless you double-tap.

  4. DrainedBack to sleep

    Drawing almost nothing until the next bath.

Questions

Good to know.

Will it work on my tub?

brim needs a smooth, flat-ish wall for the suction cups: acrylic, enamel, steel or glazed tile. Textured surfaces and grout lines don't hold suction well.

Do I need to make an account?

No. Alerts go through the free ntfy app using a private random topic that brim creates for you. There's no brim cloud and no subscription.

What if my Wi-Fi goes down mid-bath?

brim still flashes red and beeps at your full line, so you'll hear it if you're nearby. The phone alerts resume when Wi-Fi comes back.

Can someone else change its settings?

Changes need a double-tap on brim itself while it sits on its charger. Nobody on your network can change where alerts go or update it without touching it.

Does it work with bubble bath and bath oils?

Yes. It reads the water's capacitance through the wall, and a reference pad above the water line cancels film and temperature drift.

Is it a safety device?

No. brim is a reminder to turn off the tap. Never leave a child, or anyone who needs supervision, alone in the bath.
