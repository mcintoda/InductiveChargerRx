Set up once

Ten minutes to set up, then you forget it's there.

  1. Stick it at your full line

    Two suction cups hold the bracket on the inside of the tub. Put its top a few millimetres below the level you like. brim slides in from the top.

  2. Join your Wi-Fi

    Your phone joins brim's setup network and a page opens by itself. Pick your Wi-Fi, subscribe in the free ntfy app, and you'll get a “brim is connected” alert.

  3. Get the kids ready

    brim wakes when the water reaches it and watches the level rise while you sort out towels and pyjamas. Your phone tells you when to turn off the tap.

Not too hot

Set to 38 °C out of the box. If the water is warmer, your phone gets an alert and brim breathes magenta. You can also be told when the bath cools down. Check with your elbow or a thermometer before anyone gets in.

sealed

Nothing to fiddle with

No buttons, ports or switches. Rounded edges, a screwed-shut case, and a light kids can read: green good, red stop.

tilt alert

Pulled off? You’ll know

Little hands will lift it out of its bracket. That’s fine: brim is waterproof, and if it ends up somewhere it shouldn’t be, your phone says so.

Our one rule: brim watches the water, never your child.

It’s for the few minutes while the tub fills, when no one is in it. A child can slip under silently in seconds, in very little water. Once they’re in the bath, stay within arm’s reach, every time.

What's inside

Sealed shut. Senses straight through the wall.

No holes, no buttons, no ports. Everything happens through 1.2 mm of plastic.

6 levels · 60 mm

Measures, doesn't guess

Six capacitive pads read the water through the case, plus a dry reference pad that cancels drift. Suds and splashes don't fool it.

±0.1 °C sensor

Too-hot warning

A precision temperature sensor reads the water through a thin window in the case. Set the limit lower for little ones: 37–38 °C is a common target for babies.

Qi

Charges like a phone

Lay it on any flat Qi pad, or on the back of many Samsung Galaxy and Google Pixel phones. One charge lasts about six months of daily baths.

ntfy

Alerts without an account

Notifications go through ntfy, a free open-source app. No brim account, no subscription, and you can run your own server.

light + beeper

Works when Wi-Fi doesn't

brim also flashes and beeps on its own, so a full bath gets noticed even if your phone is in another room.

double-tap

One gesture

Double-tap the case to silence an alarm. Tap a sleeping brim to see its battery colour.

Questions

Good to know.

I don’t have a wireless charger. Can my phone charge it?

Probably, if it’s a recent Samsung or Pixel. Turn on Wireless PowerShare (Samsung Galaxy S10 and later S series, Galaxy Z Fold and Z Flip) or Battery Share (Google Pixel 5, 6, 7, 8 and 9 series, including Pro models), then lay brim back to back with the phone, its “Qi” ring over the middle of the phone. Samsung stops sharing below 30 % battery, and sharing is slower than a pad, so a full top-up takes a few hours. iPhones can’t share power with other devices. Any flat Qi pad works too, and you’ll only need it about twice a year.

Will it work on my tub?

brim needs a smooth, flat-ish wall for the suction cups: acrylic, enamel, steel or glazed tile. Textured surfaces and grout lines don't hold suction well.

Do I need to make an account?

No. Alerts go through the free ntfy app using a private random topic that brim creates for you. There's no brim cloud and no subscription.

What if my Wi-Fi goes down mid-bath?

brim still flashes red and beeps at your full line, so you'll hear it if you're nearby. The phone alerts resume when Wi-Fi comes back.

Can someone else change its settings?

Changes need a double-tap on brim itself while it sits on its charger. Nobody on your network can change where alerts go or update it without touching it.

Does it work with bubble bath and bath oils?

Yes. It reads the water's capacitance through the wall, and a reference pad above the water line cancels film and temperature drift.

Can I leave my child in the bath while brim watches?

No. brim only knows about the water, and it’s meant for while the tub fills, with no one in it. Children can slip under silently in seconds. Once they’re in the bath, stay within arm’s reach and keep your eyes on them, every time. brim is not a safety or monitoring device.

What if my kids pull it off the wall?

It slides out of its bracket by design, and the sealed case doesn’t mind being dunked or dropped. If it’s tilted or out of the bracket while the bath fills, your phone gets “brim is tilted”. As with any gadget that has a battery inside, don’t let little ones chew on it or take it to bed.

What temperature should I set?

brim starts at 38 °C, since many parents aim for about 37–38 °C for babies and young children. On the settings page you can pick 40 or 42 °C for older kids and adults, or any value, and add an optional “cooling down” alert. brim’s alert is a backup: always test the water yourself before anyone gets in.

How do I know brim is still working?

It checks in every two days and sends a silent “brim OK” with its battery level once a week. If it ever goes quiet for three days, because the battery is flat or it lost your Wi-Fi, the alert service sends you “brim hasn’t checked in” on its own, so a dead brim never looks like a quiet evening.
